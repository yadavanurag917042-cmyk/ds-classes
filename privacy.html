Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DS CLASSES, operated under the name DS CLASSES by Gulab Sir (“we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information when you use the DS CLASSES website or app.

Information we handle

  • Google sign-in information: when you choose Google Sign-In, Google and our authentication provider process the basic account information needed to authenticate you, such as your Google account identifier, email address, and available name. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Student profile information: a name, phone number, and address you provide during registration, plus your email address, profile photo if you upload one, and batch assignment.
  • Learning and community information: notices and class activity associated with your batch, community posts and photos you submit, and comments you send in live-class chat.
  • Authentication data: session information stored by the app and its authentication provider to keep you signed in and secure your account.

How information is used

We use this information to authenticate accounts, maintain student profiles, assign students to batches, show class notices and study materials, provide live-class and community features, and protect student and administrator access.

Google user data

Google account information obtained through Sign-In is used only for account authentication and the DS CLASSES features described in this Policy. We do not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. We do not share it with third parties except as needed to provide authentication and hosting through our service providers, to meet legal obligations, or to protect the service and its users.

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

When information is visible to others

Your phone number, address, and email are intended to be visible only to you and authorized DS CLASSES administrators. In batch community features, other eligible members may see your display name, profile photo, posts, post photos, and live-chat comments. Do not post information you do not want shared with your batch.

Service providers and external links

DS CLASSES uses Supabase for authentication, database, and file storage, and loads the Supabase client library from jsDelivr. Google provides Google Sign-In. These providers process information as needed to provide their services under their own terms and privacy policies. Class or recording links may open third-party websites; their practices are not controlled by us.

Storage, security, and retention

Profile and learning information is stored in the DS CLASSES project hosted with Supabase. The app uses access controls and private file storage for student information and learning files. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

We keep information while an account is active or as needed to operate the learning service. To request access, correction, or deletion, contact us using the privacy contact below. We may retain information where required by law or for legitimate service, safety, or dispute-resolution purposes.

Children and guardians

Students who are not legally able to consent to data processing where they live should use the service with the involvement of a parent or legal guardian. A parent or guardian may contact us with a privacy request.

Changes and contact
